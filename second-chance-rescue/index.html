The process

How adoption works

Our counselors help you find the right match, for you and for them.

  1. Browse

    Look through our dogs and cats and note the ones you'd like to meet.

  2. Get in touch

    Call or email our adoption team with questions, about cats, kids, other pets or anything else.

  3. Meet

    Visit in person. Many of our shy ones shine in the play yard, not the kennel.

  4. Apply & go home

    Submit your application, finalize with a counselor and welcome your new family member.
